# Privacy Policy for Paradi Group Management Dashboard

**Last Updated:** October 29, 2024

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Paradi Group Management Dashboard ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our Roblox OAuth 2.0 application for group management purposes.

By using our service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information Collected Through Roblox OAuth 2.0

When you authenticate with our application using Roblox OAuth 2.0, we collect:

- **Roblox User ID**: Your unique Roblox user identifier
- **Username**: Your Roblox username
- **Display Name**: Your Roblox display name
- **Profile Information**: Avatar, account creation date, and other publicly available profile data
- **Group Membership Information**: Your group memberships, roles, and permissions
- **Group Management Data**: Information about groups you manage or have administrative access to

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We may automatically collect:

- **Authentication Tokens**: OAuth 2.0 access tokens and refresh tokens (encrypted and securely stored)
- **Usage Data**: Log data including IP address, browser type, access times, and pages viewed
- **Session Information**: Data about your interactions with the dashboard

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for the following group management purposes:

### 3.1 Primary Uses

- **Authentication and Authorization**: Verifying your identity and managing your access to the dashboard
- **Group Management Functions**: 
  - Viewing and managing group members
  - Assigning and modifying member roles
  - Monitoring group activity and statistics
  - Processing group-related administrative tasks
- **Dashboard Functionality**: Providing personalized dashboard features based on your group permissions
- **Service Improvement**: Analyzing usage patterns to enhance the dashboard's performance and features

### 3.2 We Do NOT Use Your Information For

- Marketing or promotional purposes
- Selling or renting to third parties
- Any purposes outside of group management functionality
- Tracking you across other websites or services

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Data Storage

- All data is stored securely on encrypted servers
- OAuth tokens are encrypted using industry-standard encryption methods
- Access to stored data is restricted to authorized personnel only

### 4.2 Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures including:

- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Encryption of sensitive data at rest
- Regular security audits and updates
- Secure authentication protocols
- Limited access controls

### 4.3 Data Retention

- Active session data is retained while you use the service
- OAuth tokens are retained until you revoke access or for a maximum of 90 days
- Log data is retained for up to 30 days for security and operational purposes
- We delete or anonymize data when it is no longer necessary for group management purposes

## 5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

### 5.1 Limited Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

- **Roblox Platform**: Communication with Roblox API for authentication and group management operations
- **Service Providers**: Trusted third-party service providers who assist in operating our service (e.g., hosting providers) under strict confidentiality agreements
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and safety

### 5.2 Third-Party Services

Our application integrates with:

- **Roblox OAuth 2.0 API**: For authentication and authorization
- **Roblox Groups API**: For group management functionality

These services are governed by Roblox's own privacy policies and terms of service.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:

- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data we hold
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
- **Revocation**: Revoke OAuth access at any time through Roblox account settings or our dashboard
- **Data Portability**: Request your data in a portable format

### 6.2 Revoking Access

To revoke our application's access to your Roblox account:

1. Visit your Roblox account settings
2. Navigate to "Security" → "Authorized Apps"
3. Remove "Paradi Group Management Dashboard" from the list

OR

1. Log into our dashboard
2. Navigate to "Settings" → "Privacy"
3. Click "Disconnect Roblox Account"

## 7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Roblox users may include individuals under the age of 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent
- Our service only collects information necessary for group management purposes
- We do not require children to disclose more information than necessary
- Parents/guardians have the right to review, delete, or refuse further collection of their child's information

If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13 without proper consent, please contact us immediately.

## 8. International Data Transfers
